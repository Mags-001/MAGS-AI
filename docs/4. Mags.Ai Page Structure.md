# Mags.Ai — Page Structure

**Document:** 04 — Page Structure  
**Product:** Mags.Ai  
**Status:** Approved

## 1. Homepage Structure

The V1 homepage follows this order:

1. Navbar
2. Hero
3. Problems I Solve
4. About / Meet Magudeshwaran
5. Services
6. Products / Demos
7. How I Work
8. Testimonials / Clients
9. FAQ
10. Contact / Discuss Your Business Problem
11. Footer

---

## 2. Navbar

### Left
- Mags.Ai

### Center
- Problems
- Services
- Products
- About
- How I Work

### Right
- Discuss Your Business Problem

The navbar becomes a responsive mobile menu on smaller screens.

---

## 3. Hero

Contains:

- Main headline
- Supporting text
- Primary CTA: **Discuss Your Business Problem**
- Secondary CTA: **Explore My Work**
- Futuristic visual/animation system defined in Document 2

The hero should immediately communicate the Mags.Ai positioning.

---

## 4. Problems I Solve

Interactive problem cards:

- Manual Work
- Scattered Information
- Customer Management
- No Digital Presence
- Inefficient Business Workflows
- Need for Custom Tools

Cards may later connect to relevant services or products.

---

## 5. About / Meet Magudeshwaran

Contains:

- Short introduction
- Professional/business background
- Why Mags.Ai exists
- Business + technology approach
- Personal photography
- Optional About Me link

The section introduces the person behind the Mags.Ai brand.

---

## 6. Services

Four primary service categories:

### Business Websites
Websites, catalogues, landing pages, and portfolios.

### Custom Web Applications
Internal tools, dashboards, management systems, and customer systems.

### Business Automation
Workflow automation, notifications, data processing, and repetitive-task automation.

### AI Solutions
AI assistants, AI workflows, AI integrations, and business-specific AI tools.

Each service card can contain:

**Service → Problem → Examples → CTA**

Primary service CTA:

> **Discuss This Service**

---

## 7. Products / Demos

A visual showcase of products and projects.

Each product card may contain:

- Product name
- Short description
- Problem solved
- Screenshot/GIF
- Status: Demo / Concept / Live
- View Demo button

The section must support adding genuine projects later without redesigning the page.

---

## 8. How I Work

Visual process:

**Understand → Analyse → Design → Build → Test → Improve → Launch & Support**

Desktop may use a horizontal timeline.

Mobile may use a vertical timeline.

---

## 9. Testimonials / Clients

The section exists structurally but only displays genuine content.

Potential content:

- Client name
- Business
- Testimonial
- Photo/logo where appropriate

If no genuine testimonials exist, the section may remain hidden or display a clean empty state.

No fabricated testimonials or client information.

---

## 10. FAQ

Initial FAQ topics:

- What businesses do you work with?
- What solutions do you build?
- Can you build custom solutions?
- Can you work with existing systems?
- Can you integrate AI?
- How does the process work?
- How can I discuss my business problem?

Use an expandable accordion interface.

---

## 11. Contact

Primary heading:

> **Have a Business Problem? Let's Talk.**

Contains the contact form and direct communication options.

### Required fields

- Name
- Business
- Email/WhatsApp
- Business description
- Problem

### Optional fields

- Current process
- Desired improvement
- Budget
- Preferred contact method

Direct contact options:

- WhatsApp
- Email
- LinkedIn
- Instagram

## 13. Visitor Journey

The overall page should guide visitors through:

> **Understand the Brand → Recognize Their Problem → Meet the Person → Explore Solutions → See Proof → Understand the Process → Build Trust → Contact Mags.Ai**

---

## 14. Secondary Pages

V1 may include:

- About
- Individual Product/Demo pages when required
- Terms & Conditions
- Privacy Policy
- Refund/Cancellation Policy

Problems, Services, How I Work, FAQ, and Contact remain homepage sections initially.

---

## 15. Page Structure Principle

The homepage should tell a coherent story rather than function as a collection of unrelated sections.

Every section should move the visitor closer to answering:

> **“Does Mags.Ai understand my problem, can it build a solution, and should I start a conversation?”**
