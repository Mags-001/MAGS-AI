# Mags.Ai — Website Requirements

**Document:** 03 — Website Requirements  
**Product:** Mags.Ai  
**Status:** Approved  
**Purpose:** Define the functional requirements, content areas, interactions, and V1 scope of the Mags.Ai website.

---

# 1. Website Objective

The primary purpose of the Mags.Ai website is to turn visitors into genuine business conversations and potential clients.

A visitor should be able to:

1. Understand who Magudeshwaran is and what Mags.Ai does.
2. Understand the types of business problems Mags.Ai can solve.
3. Explore examples of products and digital solutions.
4. Understand how Mags.Ai approaches a business problem.
5. Contact Mags.Ai to discuss their own business problem.

The website should communicate:

> **Mags.Ai understands the business problem first and builds the appropriate digital solution second.**

---

# 2. Target Visitors

The primary V1 audience is:

### 1. Small Businesses

Businesses that may rely on:

- Manual processes
- Spreadsheets
- Notebooks
- Messaging applications
- Disconnected tools
- Basic or outdated online presence

### 2. Businesses Seeking Automation

Businesses looking to:

- Reduce repetitive work
- Automate workflows
- Organize information
- Improve operational efficiency
- Introduce AI into useful business processes

### 3. People Seeking Product Development

Individuals or businesses looking to transform an idea or business requirement into:

- A website
- A web application
- A business tool
- An automation
- An AI-powered product
- A custom digital solution

---

# 3. Website Structure

The V1 website should contain the following primary sections:

1. Navbar
2. Hero
3. Problems I Solve
4. About / Meet Magudeshwaran
5. Services
6. Products / Demos
7. How I Work
8. Clients / Testimonials
9. FAQ
10. Contact / Discuss Your Business Problem
11. Footer

A separate Case Studies section is **not required for V1**.

---

# 4. Navbar Requirements

The navigation should provide access to the major areas of the website.

Initial navigation may include:

- Mags.Ai logo/wordmark
- Services
- Products / Demos
- About
- How I Work
- Contact

The navigation should remain responsive across desktop, tablet, and mobile.

### Account functionality

Login and Sign Up are **not required for V1**.

They should not be presented unless a genuine account-based product is introduced in the future.

---

# 5. Hero Requirements

The hero must communicate the central Mags.Ai positioning immediately.

### Primary headline

> **Turn Business Problems Into Digital Solutions.**

### Supporting message

> I help businesses replace manual processes, scattered information, and repetitive work with practical websites, applications, automation, and AI-powered solutions.

The final wording may be refined during the content stage.

### Primary CTA

> **Discuss Your Business Problem**

### Secondary CTA

> **Explore My Work**

The hero should visually establish the Mags.Ai premium futuristic identity defined in Document 2.

---

# 6. Problems I Solve

This section should focus on **business problems**, not simply technical services.

Potential problems include:

- Manual customer follow-ups
- Information scattered across WhatsApp, notebooks, and spreadsheets
- Manual inventory tracking
- Repetitive administrative work
- Lack of a professional online presence
- Customers having difficulty discovering products or services
- Business workflows depending heavily on manual processes
- Businesses needing a custom digital tool instead of generic software
- Difficulty organizing business information
- Opportunities to introduce useful automation or AI

The section should communicate the transition:

> **Business Problem → Digital Opportunity → Practical Solution**

The exact problems may evolve as more real business use cases are discovered.

---

# 7. About / Meet Magudeshwaran

The website should introduce the person behind Mags.Ai.

The section should communicate:

- Commerce and finance background
- Interest in software engineering
- Interest in solving digital business problems
- Understanding of business operations
- Use of modern software technologies
- Use of AI technologies
- Personal motivation for combining business understanding with technology

### Positioning

The initial direction is:

> **A software engineering enthusiast solving digital business problems.**

Supporting message:

> I leverage my commerce and finance background to understand business needs and combine that understanding with modern software and AI technologies to build practical digital solutions.

The language should remain accurate and should not make unsupported claims about expertise.

---

Mags.Ai should present services around business outcomes rather than simply listing technologies.

## Business Websites

Potential offerings:

- Business websites
- Product catalogues
- Landing pages
- Portfolio websites

## Custom Web Applications

Potential offerings:

- Internal business tools
- Dashboards
- Management systems
- Customer management systems
- Custom workflow applications

## Automation

Potential offerings:

- Workflow automation
- Notifications
- Data processing
- Repetitive task automation
- Business workflow improvements

## AI Solutions

Potential offerings:

- AI assistants
- AI-powered workflows
- AI integrations
- Business-specific AI tools

The service list may expand as Mags.Ai gains practical experience and develops additional products.

---

# 9. Products / Demos

The Products/Demos section should **demonstrate capability rather than simply claim capability**.

Potential products include:

- Customer Follow-up System
- Inventory Tracker
- Business Product Catalogue
- Finance/business automation tools
- Future AI-powered tools

## Dynamic Content Requirement

The website should be designed so that new projects can be added later without requiring major redesign of the page.

Each product/demo should eventually be able to contain information such as:

- Product name
- Short description
- Business problem
- Solution
- Screenshot
- GIF/video demonstration
- Features
- Technologies used
- Live demo
- Source code where appropriate

### V1 empty state

If no genuine products are ready when the website launches, the site should not present fake client work as real work.

Instead it may:

- Display an intentional empty state
- Display clearly labelled personal/demo concepts
- Use clearly labelled placeholder content during development

The structure must make it easy to replace placeholders with genuine projects later.

---

# 10. How I Work

The website should communicate a business-first development process.

### Step 1 — Understand

Understand the business and identify the actual problem.

### Step 2 — Analyse

Understand the existing workflow, requirements, constraints, and opportunities.

### Step 3 — Design

Determine what digital solution makes sense for the business.

### Step 4 — Build

Develop the appropriate:

- Website
- Application
- Automation
- AI solution
- Custom digital product

### Step 5 — Test

Test the solution against realistic workflows and expected use cases.

### Step 6 — Improve

Collect feedback and improve the solution based on real-world use.

### Step 7 — Launch & Support

Launch the solution safely and continue improving it based on actual usage and feedback.

The process should reinforce:

> **Problem → Requirement → Solution → Build → Test → Improve → Launch**

---

# 11. Clients & Testimonials

A Clients/Testimonials area should be supported by the website architecture.

However, Mags.Ai must not create:

- Fake testimonials
- Fake client names
- Fake client logos
- Invented business results
- Invented statistics

### V1 behaviour

If there are no genuine testimonials or clients available, the section may:

- Remain hidden
- Display an intentional empty state
- Be prepared for future content

The website should allow genuine testimonials and client information to be added later without redesigning the entire website.

---

# 12. Case Studies

A dedicated Case Studies section is **not required for V1**.

Individual projects may still contain useful information such as:

> Problem → Solution → Result

within the Products/Demos section.

A dedicated case-study system can be introduced later when enough genuine projects and results exist.

---

# 13. Contact / Lead Capture

The primary conversion action is:

> **Discuss Your Business Problem**

The contact experience should make it easy for a potential client to explain what they need.

## Required fields

- Name
- Business name
- Email or WhatsApp
- What does your business do?
- What problem are you trying to solve?

## Optional fields

- Current process
- What would you like to improve?
- Budget
- Preferred contact method

The form should avoid unnecessary friction.

Additional direct contact methods may include:

- WhatsApp
- Email
- LinkedIn
- Instagram
- Phone where appropriate

---

# 14. Product & Website Interactions

The website should contain meaningful interaction rather than animation for decoration alone.

Potential V1 interactions include:

- Scroll storytelling
- Cursor effects
- Interactive service/product elements
- Contact form
- Demo request
- WhatsApp contact
- FAQ interactions
- Product demonstrations

Animations should support the design principles established in Document 2.

---

# 15. Mobile Requirements

The website must work properly across:

- Desktop
- Laptop
- Tablet
- Mobile

The mobile version should preserve the same **brand experience, information architecture, and core functionality** while adapting the interface to the device.

Mobile-specific adaptations may include:

- Responsive navigation
- Touch-friendly buttons
- Reduced visual effects where necessary
- Mobile-appropriate typography
- Optimized images and GIFs
- Removal or adaptation of cursor-dependent effects

The mobile experience should not simply be treated as a compressed desktop layout.

---

# 16. Legal & Trust Requirements

The V1 footer should provide access to:

- Terms & Conditions
- Refund/Cancellation Policy
- Contact
- Email
- WhatsApp
- LinkedIn
- Instagram

A Privacy Policy should also be included if the website collects personal information through forms, analytics, or other tracking mechanisms.

A shipping policy is **not required** unless Mags.Ai begins selling physical products.

---

# 17. Content Management Requirements

The website should be structured so that important content can be changed or expanded without rebuilding the entire interface.

The following should eventually be easy to add/update:

- Products
- Product screenshots
- Product GIFs
- Services
- Testimonials
- Client information
- Contact information
- Social links
- FAQ entries

For V1, this does **not necessarily require a full CMS or database**.

The implementation can begin with structured local content/data and evolve into a proper content management system if the business requires it.

---

The first public version should include:

- Responsive website

The following are outside V1:

- Sign Up
- Client accounts
- Client dashboard
- Client portal
- SaaS functionality
- Full CMS
- Client-specific applications
- Billing system
- Account management
- Mags.Ai platform functionality

---

# 19. Authenticity Requirement

Mags.Ai must maintain a strict distinction between:

**Real**

- Real clients
- Real projects
- Real testimonials
- Real results
- Real experience

and:

**Demo / Concept**

- Personal projects
- Experimental products
- Prototypes
- Concept applications
- AI-assisted experiments

The website should never imply that a personal project was built for a client when it was not.

---

# 20. V1 Success Criteria

The V1 website should successfully answer five questions for a first-time visitor:

### 1. Who is behind Mags.Ai?

**Magudeshwaran.**

### 2. What does Mags.Ai do?

**Turns business problems into practical digital solutions.**

### 3. What types of problems can it solve?

Websites, web applications, automation, AI solutions, and custom digital products.

### 4. Can it actually build things?

The Products/Demos section should provide evidence through real projects and interactive demonstrations as they become available.

### 5. How do I start a conversation?

The visitor can click:

> **Discuss Your Business Problem**

and submit their requirements or contact Mags.Ai directly.

---

# 21. Core Functional Principle

The Mags.Ai website should not function merely as an online résumé or collection of technical skills.

Its primary function is to create a bridge between:

> **Business Problem → Understanding → Digital Solution → Conversation**

The technology exists to support that journey.
